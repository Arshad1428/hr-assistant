# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy
Northwind Technologies Private Limited | HR-POL-003 | Effective 1 April 2026
## Section 1 — Work location categories
Every role is assigned one of three categories by the department head and recorded in the HR portal.
| Category | Office attendance | Applies to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| On-site | 5 days per week | Roles requiring physical presence or hardware handling |
| Hybrid | 3 days per week | Default for most engineering and corporate roles |
| Remote | Up to 4 days per month in office, by request | Granted by exception |

Hybrid employees must be present in the office on Tuesday and Wednesday, with the third day chosen by the team and fixed for a quarter at a time.
## Section 2 — Core hours
Irrespective of work location, all employees must be available between 11:00 and 17:00 IST on working days. Outside core hours, employees may structure their day as the role allows, provided deliverables and meeting commitments are met.
## Section 3 — Applying for a remote designation
A request for a Remote designation is made through the HR portal and requires:
Twelve months of continuous service and confirmation from probation;
A most recent performance rating of "Meets Expectations" or above;
Written endorsement from the reporting manager and approval from the department head.
Remote designations are reviewed every six months and may be withdrawn with 30 days' notice.
## Section 4 — Workspace obligations
An employee working remotely must have a broadband connection with a minimum sustained speed of 25 Mbps download and 5 Mbps upload, and a workspace that allows confidential calls to be taken without being overheard.
All work must be carried out on company-issued equipment. Use of a personal device for company work is prohibited except for the approved mobile email application.
## Section 5 — Working from outside India
Working from a location outside India requires approval from the department head and from the Legal and Tax team, obtained at least 21 days in advance. Approval is granted for a maximum of 30 days in any rolling 12-month period.
## Section 6 — Attendance
Office attendance is recorded by badge entry. Hybrid employees falling below the required attendance for two consecutive months will be reviewed and may have their category changed to On-site.
