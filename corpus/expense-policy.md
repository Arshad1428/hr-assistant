# Expense Reimbursement Policy
Northwind Technologies Private Limited | HR-POL-002 | Effective 1 April 2026
## Section 1 — General principles
An expense is reimbursable where it is necessary, reasonable, and incurred wholly for business purposes. Where an expense has both a business and a personal element, only the business portion may be claimed.
The reporting manager approves all claims up to Rs 25,000. Claims above Rs 25,000 require department head approval.
## Section 2 — Submission deadlines
Claims must be submitted through the HR portal within 30 days of the date the expense was incurred.
Claims submitted between 30 and 60 days require a written explanation and department head approval.
Claims submitted more than 60 days after the expense date will not be reimbursed.
Original receipts are required for every line item above Rs 500.
Approved claims are paid with the next monthly payroll cycle, provided they are approved by the 20th of the month.
## Section 3 — Local conveyance
Travel between the base office and a client site, or between two business locations during the working day, is reimbursable.
Travel between home and the base office is not reimbursable under any circumstances.
Cab and ride-hailing fares are reimbursable at actuals against the trip receipt.
Where a personal two-wheeler is used, a rate of Rs 6 per kilometre applies.
Where a personal car is used, a rate of Rs 14 per kilometre applies.
A log of date, origin, destination and distance must accompany any per-kilometre claim.
## Section 4 — Business meals
Meals with clients or external business partners are reimbursable where the business purpose and the names of attendees are recorded on the claim.
A limit of Rs 1,500 per person applies.
Meals with colleagues only are not reimbursable, except for approved team events authorised in advance.
Alcohol is not reimbursable under any circumstances, including as part of a client meal. Where a bill includes alcohol, that portion must be deducted before claiming.
## Section 5 — Never reimbursed
Traffic fines and parking penalties; personal grooming; fuel for commuting; late fees arising from delayed submission; and any expense claimed without a recorded business purpose.
