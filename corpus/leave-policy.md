# Leave Policy
Northwind Technologies Private Limited | HR-POL-001 | Effective 1 April 2026
## Section 1 — Applicability
This policy applies to all confirmed employees on the direct payroll. Contractors and interns are not covered. The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December.
## Section 2 — Casual Leave
Confirmed employees are entitled to 12 days of Casual Leave per leave year, credited at one day per completed month of service.
Casual Leave may be availed for personal exigencies, family obligations and religious observances. No documentation is required.
A minimum of half a day may be taken.
No more than 3 consecutive days may be taken at one time. Longer absences must be applied for as Earned Leave.
Casual Leave does not carry forward and is not encashable. Any unused balance lapses on 31 December.
## Section 3 — Sick Leave
Confirmed employees are entitled to 12 days of Sick Leave per leave year, credited in full on 1 January.
A medical certificate from a registered practitioner is required for any absence of 3 or more consecutive days. Sick Leave does not carry forward and is not encashable.
## Section 4 — Earned Leave
Confirmed employees accrue 18 days of Earned Leave per leave year, at 1.5 days per completed month of service.
Applications require 10 working days notice and written approval from the reporting manager.
A maximum of 45 days may be accumulated; accrual stops at this ceiling.
Up to 15 days carry forward into the next leave year. Any balance above 15 days as at 31 December is encashed at basic salary rate with the January payroll.
## Section 5 — Bereavement Leave
5 working days of paid Bereavement Leave are available on the death of an immediate family member — spouse, child, parent, sibling or parent-in-law. No documentation is required.
## Section 6 — Loss of Pay
Absence without an available balance, or without approval, is recorded as Loss of Pay and deducted from gross monthly salary on a pro-rata basis using a 30-day month.
